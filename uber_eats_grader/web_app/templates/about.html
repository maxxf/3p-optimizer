About 3P Delivery Optimizer

What We Do

The 3P Delivery Optimizer is a specialized tool developed by Spice Agency to help restaurants maximize their performance on third-party delivery platforms like Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Grubhub.

Our tool analyzes your restaurant's digital storefront across five key dimensions:

Menu Structure

How well your menu is organized, categorized, and presented to maximize conversions.

Pricing Strategy

How your pricing compares to competitors and whether it's optimized for the delivery market.

Customer Sentiment

What your ratings and reviews tell about customer satisfaction and areas for improvement.

Visual Presentation

How your food photography and visual elements impact customer decisions.

Promotion Strategy

How effectively you're using promotions, deals, and platform features to drive orders.

How It Works

1. Data Collection

We extract comprehensive data from your restaurant's delivery platform storefront, including menu items, pricing, images, descriptions, and customer reviews.

2. Competitive Analysis

We identify and analyze similar restaurants in your area to benchmark your performance against the competition.

3. Performance Grading

Our algorithm evaluates your performance across all key dimensions and assigns grades based on industry best practices and competitive benchmarks.

4. Actionable Recommendations

We provide specific, prioritized recommendations to improve your delivery platform performance and increase orders.
